<h2 class="pub-title"><a href="{{ '/assets/pubs/NASA-TM-20250009029.pdf' | relative_url }}">Aeropulse: A Network of Drones with Autonomous, AI-Driven Robotic Payloads to Optimize Delivery of Medical Perishables Across Urban Hospitals</a></h2>
<p class="pub-authors">F. Abdul, S. Gandhi, K. Garg, J. Gore, D. Mei and <strong>D. Piwko</strong><br><span>All six authors contributed equally and are listed alphabetically.</span></p>
<p class="pub-venue">NASA/TM-20250009029, NASA Ames Research Center, Mountain View, CA</p>
<p>Written during the Aviation Systems Division's Concept Design Experience. It proposes a network of hybrid VTOL drones and stair-climbing robots that carries organs and urgent medical supplies between urban hospitals with no manual handoffs. My part was drone routing and urban airspace integration.</p>
