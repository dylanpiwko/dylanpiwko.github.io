<h2 id="about">About</h2>

I'm a first-year aerospace engineering student at the University of Michigan.

I've been flying high-power rockets since 2023. Right now I'm designing Kestrel, a flight computer for my next rocket, which simulates to about Mach 1.7 on an L2200G.

In summer 2025 I interned at NASA Ames. Our team of six designed Aeropulse, a drone and robot system for moving organs between hospitals, and we published it as a NASA Technical Memorandum.

This fall I joined an Embry-Riddle team for the EUROCONTROL PRC Data Challenge, predicting how long planes take to taxi out at European airports. In January I'm starting on Firefly Aerospace's Eclipse catch recovery system through the Perot Jain TechLab.

I'm looking for summer 2027 internships in GNC, unmanned systems or systems engineering.

### Firefly Aerospace, Eclipse catch recovery
*Perot Jain TechLab, starting January 2027*

### High-power rocketry
*April 2023 – Present*

I'm NAR Level 1 certified and have been flying high-power rockets since 2023, including two-stage and clustered-motor builds with dual-deployment recovery. In March 2024 I flew a 3" MAC Performance Black Fly on a CTI I223SK to about 3,500 ft at Michiana Rocketry. It was my first flight where the avionics ran head-end dual deployment.

### RC aircraft and vehicles
*April 2024 – August 2026*

I build and fly electric ducted-fan and fixed-wing RC planes. I also switched an RC car from a 2S to a 4S lithium battery and re-geared it, which made it about 50% faster.
