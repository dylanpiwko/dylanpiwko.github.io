<h2 id="about">About</h2>

I'm a first-year aerospace engineering student at the University of Michigan, interested in GNC, unmanned systems and systems engineering.

I've flown high-power rockets since 2023, and I'm now designing Kestrel, a flight computer for a rocket built to reach Mach 1.7.

In summer 2025 I interned at NASA Ames, where our team of six designed Aeropulse, a drone and robot network for moving organs between hospitals, and co-authored the NASA Technical Memorandum on it. This fall I worked on taxi-time prediction with an Embry-Riddle team in the EUROCONTROL PRC Data Challenge, and in January I start on Firefly Aerospace's Eclipse catch recovery system through the Perot Jain TechLab.

I'm looking for summer 2027 internships in GNC, unmanned systems or systems engineering.

<h2 id="other">Other work</h2>

### Firefly Aerospace, Eclipse catch recovery
*Perot Jain TechLab, starting January 2027*

I was selected to work with Firefly Aerospace on the catch recovery system for the Eclipse launch vehicle.

### High-power rocketry
*April 2023 – Present*

I hold a NAR Level 1 certification and have designed, built and flown high-power rockets with multistage designs, clustered motors and dual-deployment recovery. In March 2024 I flew a 3" MAC Performance Black Fly on a CTI I223SK to about 3,500 ft at Michiana Rocketry, my first flight with head-end dual deployment controlled by avionics.

### RC aircraft and vehicles
*April 2024 – August 2026*

I've built, set up and flight-tested electric ducted-fan and fixed-wing RC aircraft. On an RC car, I went from a 2S to a 4S lithium battery and changed the gearing, which raised top speed by about 50%.
